Available Panchakarma Facilities

Panchakarma — the five-fold Ayurvedic purification — eliminates deep-seated toxins and restores the body's natural healing intelligence. We offer the full classical spectrum.

01

Snehana

Oleation & therapeutic oil massage to lubricate and prepare body tissues.

02

Svedana

Sudation / steam therapy to open channels and expel metabolic waste.

03

Vamana

Therapeutic emesis to eliminate Kapha and upper digestive toxins.

04

Virechana

Therapeutic purgation for deep Pitta cleansing and liver detoxification.

05

Basti

Medicated enema therapy — the most potent Vata-pacifying Panchakarma.

06

Nasya

Nasal administration of medicated oils for head, neck & sensory organs.

07

Raktamokshana

Bloodletting therapy to purify blood and treat Pitta-related skin conditions.

08

Shirodhara

Continuous warm oil pouring on the forehead — deeply calming for the nervous system.

09

Kati Basti

Warm oil pooling treatment for lower back pain & lumbar disorders.

10

Janu Basti

Medicated oil retention therapy for knee joint pain and arthritis.

11

Manya Basti

Therapeutic oil pooling for neck and cervical region disorders.

12

Hrid Basti

Heart region treatment with warm medicated oils for cardio-pulmonary wellness.
